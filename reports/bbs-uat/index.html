Blocker B · Crew & vessel login use email OTP (needs inbox access)

Observed: crew/vessel accounts authenticate with a 6-digit email code (AWS SES), not password, delivered to the authorized inbox. The flow renders correctly ("Send verification code" → OTP step).

Impact: the OTP flow was verified but interactive crew/vessel walkthroughs could not be completed without reading the delivered OTP. To finish that third of the suite, either (a) enable OTP delivery/reading for the test inbox, or (b) provide a password-enabled crew/vessel test account.
