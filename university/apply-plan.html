⚠ Read this first — order matters

  • You cannot submit a complete application to an IELTS-required university before you have the score. German portals reject/incomplete it. The test score must be in your hand first (~13 days after test day).
  • So: today you apply only to MOI-accepted schools. Everything test/uni-assist/VPD waits for the score.
  • uni-assist covers only your 8 programs. The other 68 are direct applications — separate portals, mostly free.
  • Fees are non-refundable — add programs deliberately, not shotgun.
