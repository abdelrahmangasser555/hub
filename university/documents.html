Documents Guide

Every document you'll need to apply. What it is, what should be in it, and whether you already have it.
You have it Action needed Plan / prepare
Bachelor's Degree / Final-Semester Proof You'll have it
What it is: proof you finished (or are about to finish) your AAST Computer Engineering degree. Since you have 1 semester left, you submit your transcript + a provisional-graduation letter from AAST confirming you're in your final semester with an expected graduation date.
  • Must include: your name, degree title, ECTS/credits completed, expected graduation date.
Transcript (official) You'll have it
What it is: your official AAST grade record. Request it, plus a credit certificate (ECTS breakdown) and the English medium-of-instruction (MOI) letter from the registrar.
  • MOI letter confirms your degree was taught in English — useful at many Fachhochschulen.
IELTS or TOEFL score Book now
What it is: your universal English key. Target IELTS Academic 6.5+ (or TOEFL iBT 90+). It unlocks every university and smooths the visa.
  • Your IGCSE English (grade 7), AAST MOI letter and Northampton UK degree are strong supporting signals — but not a universal replacement. Saarland needs C1 (IELTS 7.0+).
Cambridge IGCSE certificates You have it
What it is: your high-school credentials — Cambridge Statement of Results + O-Level grades (e.g. Reed Statement).
  • Submit the certificate + O-Level grades for your uni-assist VPD. Already in English; keep a certified copy.
Passport Verify validity
What it is: your identity document. Needs 6+ months validity beyond your stay and 2 blank pages.
  • Scan the bio-data page.
CV (Europass / tabular) Prepare
What it is: a 1–2 page résumé. Highlight your 3 years of work + clients, Northampton UK degree, and IGCSE grades.
  • Practice-oriented, scannable, no fluff. Mention projects.
Motivation letter Write per uni
What it is: 1–2 pages explaining why that university/program/Germany. Mention your UK degree and work experience.
  • Tailor separately to each university — don't mass-send one template.
uni-assist VPD Start weeks 5–6
What it is: the certificate uni-assist issues after verifying your documents (valid 1 year). Required by the unis marked "uni-assist".
  • See the uni-assist guide for the full step-by-step.
GRE (Saarland only) Conditional
What it is: a test Saarland mandates for non-EU applicants. Only take it if you seriously target Saarland.
Blocked account + visa docs Later
What it is: proof of funds (Sperrkonto, ~€11,904 for 2025/26) + visa application at the German Embassy Cairo once admitted.
