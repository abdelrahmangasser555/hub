IELTS or TOEFL score Book now
What it is: your universal English key. Target IELTS Academic 6.5+ (or TOEFL iBT 90+). Unlocks every university and smooths the visa. Result ~13 days after the test — book first.
  • IGCSE English (grade 7), AAST MOI letter and your Northampton UK degree are strong supporting signals but not a universal replacement. Saarland needs C1 (IELTS 7.0+).
Book IELTS (IDP Egypt) →British Council Egypt →TOEFL →
Bachelor's Degree / Final-Semester Proof You'll have it
What it is: proof you finished (or are finishing) your AAST Computer Engineering degree. With 1 semester left, submit your transcript + a provisional-graduation letter from AAST confirming your final semester and expected graduation date.
  • Must include: name, degree title, ECTS/credits completed, expected graduation date.
Transcript (official) You'll have it
What it is: your official AAST grade record. Request it, plus the credit certificate (ECTS breakdown) and the English medium-of-instruction (MOI) letter from the registrar.
  • MOI letter confirms your degree was taught in English — useful at many Fachhochschulen.
AAST registrar →
English medium-of-instruction (MOI) letter You have it
What it is: an official AAST letter confirming your bachelor's was taught in English. Together with your Northampton UK degree this is your MOI proof for the 16 MOI-accepting universities.
  • Works where the program says "MOI accepted". Not accepted at the 17 TEST universities.
Cambridge IGCSE certificates You have it
What it is: your high-school credentials — Cambridge Statement of Results + O-Level grades. Already in English.
  • Submit the certificate + O-Level grades for your uni-assist VPD. Keep a certified copy.
Passport Verify validity
What it is: your identity document. Needs 6+ months validity beyond your stay and 2 blank pages.
  • Scan the bio-data page.
CV (Europass / tabular) Prepare
What it is: a 1–2 page résumé. Highlight your 3 years of work + clients, Northampton UK degree, and IGCSE grades.
  • Practice-oriented, scannable, no fluff. Mention projects.
Europass CV maker →
Motivation letter Write per uni
What it is: 1–2 pages explaining why that university/program/Germany. Mention your UK degree and work experience.
  • Tailor separately to each university — don't mass-send one template.
uni-assist VPD Start with IELTS
What it is: the certificate uni-assist issues after verifying your documents (valid 1 year). Needed by unis marked "uni-assist" and by TU Berlin.
  • See the uni-assist guide for the full step-by-step and the 8-program checklist.
