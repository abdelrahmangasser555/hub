Read this first

Rules change. This page reflects the procedures as of 2026 and is a personal planning guide, not legal advice. Confirm every step with the German embassy/cultural section and the Egyptian missions (بعثات) office before acting — especially anything involving the visa and the military service.

1 · Which document unlocks what

⚠ The professor's "invitation letter" is a reference — not admission

What you already have: a reference/recommendation letter from a professor, an English-MOI certificate, a transcript (GPA + credits), and IGCSE school results.

What actually unlocks the visa and the deferral: a formal admission offer (Zulassungsbescheid) from a recognized German university. A reference letter helps you get admission, but it is not the admission itself. The chain is admission → blocked account → student visa → deferral follows from being a registered student — never the reverse.

2 · College application — the steps
1
Pick programs that fit you
English-taught M.Sc. in Computer Science / Informatics, open admission (no GPA cut-off), tuition-free, and that accept your MOI certificate + IGCSE English in place of IELTS. Filter the report to free & open for the best odds.
2
uni-assist account + VPD
Create an account at my.uni-assist.de. Submit your documents to get a VPD (pre-check of your Egyptian/Arab + UK degree) — this normally takes 4–6 weeks, so start it early. This is the recognition step that German unis use to judge if your bachelor's qualifies.
3
Submit applications by deadline
Apply for the intakes you target: Winter 2027/28 (apply by ~Jul 15 2027, starts early Oct) and/or Summer 2027 (apply by ~Jan 2027, starts early Apr). Follow each uni's own portal (direct) or via uni-assist.
4
Handle conditional admission
With one semester left, most programs can issue a conditional admission (bedingte Zulassung) pending your final degree. Confirm the program accepts this before counting on it. You may also need to prove you will graduate.
5
Fund the blocked account
Open a Sperrkonto (blocked account) — ~€11,904/yr for 2026 — required for the student visa. Plan for housing + health insurance too.
6
Apply for the student visa
With the admission + blocked account, file the national (D) student visa at the German embassy. English proof via your MOI certificate is generally accepted at visa stage.
3 · Military-service deferral (تأجيل التجنيد)

The good news

Studying a master's abroad defers your conscription until age 29 — it's the deferral cap, not a flat "2 years" (your actual study period governs). The route runs through the Egyptian
educational missions (البعثات) → conscription office (إدارة التجنيد — حِلمية الزيتون) → a travel permit (تصريح سفر) valid Aug–Aug. Since 2025–2026 the missions office generally wants to see the student visa in hand too.

1
Secure admission + visa first
Get your German admission and student visa before the military file. The deferral is a consequence of being an accepted student abroad, not a separate thing you "obtain".
2
Register with المبعثات (missions)
File your student file (قيد, admission, visa, passport, graduation docs) at the Egyptian educational missions office to put yourself under scientific supervision abroad.
3
Conscription office (إدارة التجنيد)
They issue the deferral letter (إفادة تأجيل). Master's deferrals run to age 29.
4
Travel permit (تصريح السفر)
Return to المبعثات with the deferral letter → they route you to تجنيد once more for the travel permit. It's valid for one year (recommended Aug–Aug) and used to leave Egypt.
5
Renew each year
Renew the deferral + permit each academic year with a current enrollment (قيد) certificate, attested (تصديق) through the Egyptian cultural bureau.
4 · The conditions that make or break it

⚠ The big one — only ONE institution defers you

You can only be deferred by one institution at a time. If AAST is currently deferring you as an enrolled student, a German university cannot also defer you. Once your German admission is secure, you must withdraw your enrollment (سحب القيد) from AAST in Egypt — otherwise the missions office will reject your file. Get the German admission first, then withdraw AAST, then file the deferral.

Bottom line

Start uni-assist now — your documents are the real core, and no IELTS is fine with your MOI certificate. Sequence strictly: admission → funds → visa → withdraw AAST → deferral → travel permit → enroll. Do the formal steps with المبعثات and the embassy so nothing stalls on the single-deferral rule.
