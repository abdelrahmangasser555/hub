✅ Fully digital — no paper post

uni-assist's official FAQ: "You do not need to send documents to uni-assist by post. The application is fully digital — upload all required documents in My assist." Upload each scanned document once; uni-assist reuses it across all your programs.

What is uni-assist?

uni-assist (UNI-ASSIST e.V., Berlin) is the central service that verifies international documents for German universities. It works two ways:

1 · Full application — you submit everything through uni-assist and they forward it to the university. Example: Rostock, Halle, Dresden, Leipzig, Anhalt, Flensburg.

2 · VPD — uni-assist verifies your degree and issues a certificate (valid 1 year) you then attach to a direct application on the university's own portal. Example: TU Berlin.

Egypt does not need the APS certificate — you use uni-assist's verification instead.

⚠ Order matters

You cannot submit a complete application to an IELTS-required program before you have the score. Take IELTS first (result ~2 weeks), then add all 8 uni-assist programs in one batch. See the Apply Calendar for the exact day-by-day order.

Register & submit — the steps
1
Go to my.uni-assist.de
Click Register. Name must match your passport exactly; Egyptian nationality; your DOB.
2
Verify your email
Click the confirmation link to activate the account.
3
Complete your profile
Education history: Cambridge IGCSE high-school, then AAST bachelor's. Add English proficiency.
4
Add the universities & programs
Dashboard → Add university/course → pick each uni and the exact program above. Add all 8 in one semester cycle so the €75 first fee is paid once.
5
Upload scans of every document
passport · IGCSE/O-Level · bachelor's · transcript · IELTS/TOEFL (for test-required) · CV · motivation letters. Uploaded once, used for all 8.
6
Pay the fee
First application €75, each additional €30 (per semester). Keep the payment reference.
7
Submit & wait 4–6 weeks
Check My Inbox regularly; respond to missing-document requests fast.
8
Get VPD or forwarded application
VPD: appears in My Inbox (valid 1 yr) → attach to direct university application. Full: uni-assist forwards your file; decision comes via uni-assist / the university.

Direct-portal universities (not via uni-assist) cost €0 to apply. IELTS exam ≈ €230. Fees are non-refundable even on rejection.

Pitfalls
  • Name must match your passport on every document — mismatches cause rejections.
  • Batch all 8 in one cycle to avoid repaying the €75 first fee per semester.
  • Start 4–6 weeks early. Missing the VPD deadline = missing the whole intake.
  • Keep the payment reference — proof of payment is often required.
  • IELTS-required programs (Rostock, Halle) won't clear your application until the score is uploaded — take IELTS first.
