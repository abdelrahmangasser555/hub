uni-assist Guide

Everything about VPD: registering, applying, what it costs, and pitfalls. Read this before you do anything.
Start here first. uni-assist processing takes 4–6 weeks, so your VPD applications go in during weeks 5–6 of your timeline (~Oct 19 – Nov 1). Do not start the 3-month plan before you understand this page.

What is uni-assist?

uni-assist is a central German service (run by UNI-ASSIST e.V. in Berlin) that verifies international school & university documents for German universities. It works two ways:

  • VPD (Vorprüfungsdokumentation): they check your documents and issue a certificate (valid 1 year). You then apply on the university's own portal and upload that VPD. Example: TU Berlin.
  • Full application: you submit everything through uni-assist and they forward it to the university. Example: Rostock, Halle.

Who needs it: students with non-German credentials, as an alternative to the APS. Egypt does not need APS — you need the uni-assist VPD instead (for the universities that require it).

Register (my.uni-assist.de)

1
Go to my.uni-assist.de
Click Register and fill: your email, full name (must match your passport exactly), date of birth, nationality (Egyptian), and contact details.
2
Verify your email
They send a confirmation link. Click it to activate the account.
3
Complete your profile
Fill in your education history: Cambridge IGCSE high-school, then your AAST Bachelor's. Add English proficiency details.
4
Add the universities & programs
Dashboard → Add university / course. Add each uni and the exact program (e.g. "Computer Science – M.Sc."). Batch them all in one semester cycle so you pay the €75 first fee once, not per semester.

Upload & submit

5
Upload scans of every required document
Passport, IGCSE/O-Level certificate, bachelor's transcript, credit certificate, IELTS/TOEFL, CV, motivation letters. Upload originals; attach certified translations where asked. uni-assist tells you exactly what's missing.
6
Pay the fee
First application €75, each additional €30 (per semester). Pay by card or bank transfer and keep your reference number.
7
Submit & wait 4–6 weeks
They check completeness + authenticity. Check My Inbox regularly — they message if a document is missing. Respond fast.
8
Get your VPD (or forwarded application)
VPD path: it appears in My Inbox (valid 1 year) → upload it with your direct application to the uni.
Full path: uni-assist forwards your file; your decision comes through uni-assist / the university.

Cost — what money you need

First uni-assist application€75
Each additional university (same cycle)€30
Typical realistic batch (~5 uni-assist unis)≈ €195
Total application budget (incl. IELTS + docs)≈ €300–500

Direct-portal universities (not via uni-assist) cost €0 to apply. IELTS exam ~ €230. Document legalisation + sworn translations ~ €50–150.

Pitfalls

  • Your name must match your passport on every document — mismatches cause rejections.
  • Batch all uni-assist applications in one cycle to avoid repaying the €75 first fee.
  • Start 4–6 weeks early. Missing the VPD deadline means missing the whole intake.
  • Keep the payment reference — proof you paid is often required.
